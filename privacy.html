Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

This policy describes the MMLedger mobile app, operated by Alpha Solution Communication. Support and privacy contact: contact@asc-mm.com.

Google sign-in: information accessed and why

If you choose Sign in with Google, Google provides basic account information to Supabase, our authentication provider. This includes your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information such as your name and profile photo URL when available. Supabase processes this information as authentication and account metadata and issues a session that allows the app to recognize your signed-in account.

We use this information to create or identify your MMLedger account, sign you in, and associate your account with your saved expense records and preferences. Basic profile information may be used to identify your account. MMLedger does not receive your Google password.

The app uses Google for sign-in only. It does not request permission to read your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Calendar, or Google Contacts.

Storage, sharing, and use of Google account information

Google account information is processed and stored by Supabase as part of providing authentication and hosting for MMLedger. The app retains authentication session information on your device to keep you signed in. Account access controls and HTTPS connections help protect this information.

We use Google account information only to provide the sign-in and account features described in this policy. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence models. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Household members can see your MMLedger profile name when you participate in a shared household. Google authentication credentials and session tokens are not shared with household members. Google and Supabase also process technical and security information in operating their services.

Your Google connection and deletion choices

You can remove MMLedger's Google connection through your Google Account connections settings. Removing that connection does not itself delete records already stored in your MMLedger account.

To delete your MMLedger account and associated records from the live service, use Settings → Delete account in the app, or follow our account-deletion instructions to request help at contact@asc-mm.com. Google-derived account information is retained while your account is active and is removed with your authentication account when account deletion is completed. Technical and authentication logs may remain under Supabase's retention settings, as explained below.

Information you provide

MMLedger stores your account email and profile name, language and currency preferences, wallet names and balances, expenses, categories, merchants, notes, and household memberships and invitations. Only enter information you want to store in your account.

How information is used

We use this information to sign you in, synchronize your records, calculate reports, and share household records with household members. MMLedger does not connect to your bank or move money. Wallet balances are records you enter yourself.

Household sharing

Members can view records in household wallets and member profile names. Keep personal records in personal wallets. Joining a household grants its members access to the information you place in its shared wallets.

Service providers and permissions

Supabase provides account authentication and database hosting. Apple and Google process sign-in when you choose their login options. The camera is used only to scan household invitation QR codes. MMLedger does not save or upload QR camera frames or provide receipt OCR. The Android QR scanner uses Google ML Kit; iOS uses Apple APIs. Service providers may process technical connection and security logs under their own privacy terms.

Advertising and tracking

The current version does not include advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Records remain while your account is active or until removed. Ordinary expense deletion hides the entry from the app and retains it in the database. Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account and its associated records from the live database. This removes households you own, your wallets and expenses, your custom categories and merchants, and balance entries you submitted, including those in shared wallets. Other members’ wallets remain but may become personal wallets when a household is removed. Technical and authentication logs are processed by Supabase under its service retention settings and may remain after account deletion; deleting an account does not immediately erase those logs.

Security and your choices

Connections use HTTPS and account access controls. No system is completely secure. You can edit your profile and records, leave households, deny camera access, or delete your account. The app can be used for manual expense entry without camera access.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published with the app and at its public privacy-policy URL. For support, privacy questions, or account deletion without access to the app, email contact@asc-mm.com. Never send your password.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

This policy describes the MMLedger mobile app, operated by Alpha Solution Communication. Support and privacy contact: contact@asc-mm.com.

Information you provide

MMLedger stores your account email and profile name, language and currency preferences, wallet names and balances, expenses, categories, merchants, notes, and household memberships and invitations. Only enter information you want to store in your account.

How information is used

We use this information to sign you in, synchronize your records, calculate reports, and share household records with household members. MMLedger does not connect to your bank or move money. Wallet balances are records you enter yourself.

Household sharing

Members can view records in household wallets and member profile names. Keep personal records in personal wallets. Joining a household grants its members access to the information you place in its shared wallets.

Service providers and permissions

Supabase provides account authentication and database hosting. Apple and Google process sign-in when you choose their login options. The camera is used only to scan household invitation QR codes. MMLedger does not save or upload QR camera frames or provide receipt OCR. The Android QR scanner uses Google ML Kit; iOS uses Apple APIs. Service providers may process technical connection and security logs under their own privacy terms.

Advertising and tracking

The current version does not include advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Records remain while your account is active or until removed. Ordinary expense deletion hides the entry from the app and retains it in the database. Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account and its associated records from the live database. This removes households you own, your wallets and expenses, your custom categories and merchants, and balance entries you submitted, including those in shared wallets. Other members’ wallets remain but may become personal wallets when a household is removed. We do not keep manual database backups or user-data exports. Our current Supabase Free plan does not include scheduled project backups. Technical and authentication logs are processed by Supabase under its service retention settings and may remain after account deletion; deleting an account does not immediately erase those logs.

Security and your choices

Connections use HTTPS and account access controls. No system is completely secure. You can edit your profile and records, leave households, deny camera access, or delete your account. The app can be used for manual expense entry without camera access.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published with the app and at its public privacy-policy URL. For support, privacy questions, or account deletion without access to the app, email contact@asc-mm.com. Never send your password.

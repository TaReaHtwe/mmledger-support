MMLedger — everyday expense tracking

MMLedger is a mobile expense tracker operated by Alpha Solution Communication. Record your spending, organize wallets, and understand personal and household expenses in English and Myanmar.

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

What you can do with MMLedger

  • Record expenses with amounts, currencies, categories, merchants, dates, and notes.
  • Organize personal wallets and view spending reports.
  • Create or join a household and share records in household wallets with its members.
  • Choose English or Myanmar and manage your display currency preferences.

Why MMLedger offers Google sign-in

Google sign-in lets you create or access your MMLedger account so your expense records can be synchronized across your devices. It does not give MMLedger access to your bank account.

When you choose Google sign-in, Google shares basic account information with our authentication provider, Supabase: your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information such as your name and profile photo URL when available. This information is used to authenticate you and maintain your MMLedger account.

MMLedger does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Calendar, or Google Contacts. Your Google password is entered on Google's sign-in page; MMLedger does not receive it.

You can read this website and our privacy policy without signing in. An account is required to use the app's synchronized expense-record features.

Delete your MMLedger account

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

You can permanently delete your account and associated records.

Inside the app

  1. Sign in to MMLedger.
  2. Open Settings → Delete account.
  3. Read the shared-household warning, type DELETE, and confirm.
  4. For an Apple-linked account, authorize Apple access revocation when prompted.

Without the app

Email contact@asc-mm.com with the subject “MMLedger account deletion request”. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Use the email associated with your account. We will verify account ownership before deleting data. Never send your password or a login verification code.

What is removed

Your authentication account, profile, owned wallets and households, expenses (including hidden deleted entries), custom categories and merchants, invitation and membership records, and balance entries you submitted are removed from the live database.

Effects on household members

Households you own are removed. Other members’ wallets remain and become personal wallets. Their own expense records remain; references to your deleted custom categories and merchants are cleared. Shared balances may change when your submitted checkpoints are deleted. Tell your household members before continuing.

Backups and logs

We do not keep manual database backups or user-data exports. Our current Supabase Free plan does not include scheduled project backups. Technical and authentication logs are processed by Supabase under its service retention settings and may remain after account deletion; deleting an account does not immediately erase those logs.
